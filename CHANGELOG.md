# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — the ride

- Full shell cycle: tube → ignition → climb → apex → flash → shock-sphere → bloom → fall → breather.
- Shell repertoire: golden willow, chrysanthemum, palm, crossette, ring, strobe, grand finale.
- Time-dilation hero control (×1 → ×15) with free-fly inside the bloom.
- Seven seats: tube, apex, bloom, crossette second generation, ring halo, below the willow, promenade.
- Procedural harbour: promenade and bridge crowd, moored boats, ferris wheel, water reflections,
  flash-lit facades with correct flash lag.
- Quality tiers with automatic step-down and DPR clamped to 2.

## v0.9.0 — the hold

- Analytic ember integration moved fully into the vertex shader; lifetime pooling rewrites only the
  dirty attribute ranges.

## v0.5.0 — the climb

- Two-stage launch ramp tuned as a curve of velocity, not a keyframe list; camera flinch series.

## v0.1.0 — the tube

- Mortar interior, fuse flicker, crowd glow over the lip, ignition fire.
